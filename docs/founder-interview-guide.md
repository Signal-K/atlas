# Atlas founder interview guide (ASV-29)

## Goal

Get the willingness-to-pay and jobs-to-be-done context PostHog can't invent
at Atlas's current traffic (~36 visitors/2 weeks). This is a human task --
the questions and competitive price notes below are prep; the 10-15 actual
conversations still need to happen and get logged here or in Craft.

## Interview guide (5 questions max)

1. When did you last plan a night out under the stars -- what did that
   actually involve?
2. What have you paid for around that (an app, a guided tour, gear)? What
   did it cost?
3. If Atlas existed then, would it have replaced that spend, or sat
   alongside it?
4. What's the one feature that would make you pay for something like this?
5. (Optional, if pricing comes up naturally) What would feel like a fair
   one-time or monthly price for that?

Keep it conversational -- these are chats, not a survey script. Don't read
the questions verbatim if the conversation is already covering the ground.

## Where to find people

Astronomy club members, EIA/alumni network, dark-sky community, founder
circles -- per the ticket, 10-15 short chats is the target.
