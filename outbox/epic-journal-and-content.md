# Atlas journal and editorial content

Make Atlas feel like an ongoing observing companion rather than only a
tonight utility. This epic has two complementary surfaces:

1. A richer private Journal/Scrapbook where observations become durable,
   revisitable memories, including unsuccessful attempts.
2. An in-app editorial feed from The Daily Transit (TDT), giving users a
   reason to return for astronomy, exploration and people stories between
   observing sessions.

This epic is deliberately limited to the Atlas app. Booking, tour CRM,
partner operations, event inventory, poster production and social publishing
belong to the separate experiences/events product. Atlas may consume official
tour metadata later so tour observations can receive a portfolio/event
context and city or venue stamps.

## Child stories

- story-journal-rich-entry-editor
- story-daily-transit-in-app-feed

## Boundary

The Journal and TDT feed can link to official experiences and provide
attribution hooks, but they must not become the booking system or operational
source of truth for events.
